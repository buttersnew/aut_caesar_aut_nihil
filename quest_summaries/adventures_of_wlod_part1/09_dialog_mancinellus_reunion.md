Mancinellus: "Ave!"

Wlodowiecus: "Mancinellus!? I didn't expect to find you again, you useless bastard!"

Mancinellus: "Greetings! What a warm welcome! Haha, it was taking me a while to find you Wlodowiecus!"

Wlodowiecus: "All of this is your fault! Because of you, we were imprisoned and nearly thrown into a quarry as slaves! However, I do have a more pressing question... How you survived? And how did you managed to find us?"

Mancinellus: "Oh well... Let me explain my good friend! When I saw Karishma, it was love in first sight. I never saw such a beautiful dark skinned woman in my life, I showered her with compliments and she made me her lover. But here's where my tale turns sour... Soon I discovered that I was not allowed to leave her palace. In fact, I was imprisoned in her room. She spent the whole day together with me and ignored her advisors and all other important people in her court. How reckless, right? But I don't blame her, I am pretty amazing! I digress, one day I discovered that the army of Emperor Kujula Kadphises was preparing to lay siege to Sagala but she just ignored it thinking her walls could hold them back. As I heard his army is the largest in the world I thought it would be wise to switch sides so... I wrote a letter and told a servant to bring it to Kujula. I don't want to get into much detail but for several days I had some nice correspondence with him. When the prison break happened, I informed him and he decided it would be the best moment to attack. So they stormed the town and killed the population but not me nor Karishma."

Wlodowiecus: "You are a horny bastard; you know this right?"

Hadrianus: "Ach, I feel sorry for the poor citizens. Why did they had to die?"

Mancinellus: "I don't know, but most importantly I am alive! I asked Kujula if I could marry Karishma and he agreed. I also asked him for the marble and he said he will send a large caravan to Rome and we could join it. And for my help he also gifted me the marble and a lot of spices. I will sell them in Rome for a high price, haha!"

Wlodowiecus: "Given the fact that it was you fault you should do the hounorable and share your spoils with us."

Mancinellus: "Of course my good friend! You can even sleep with Karishma if you like. But only if she agrees to it through."

Hadrianus: "Uh... for my part want a share of the spices... and not Karishma."

Wlodowiecus: "I was also speaking about the spices and not... gah! You are such a horny bastard!"

Mancinellus: "Not Karishma? Are you sure my friend? I am pretty sure she would sleep with you guys, maybe even with all of us together! She has a preference for white men and I must admit that she's like a tiger in a bed! I barely managed to keep up with her that week she had me locked in her bedroom... But I digress again... I will divide the spices in a fair way for you my friends.

Also... there is a new face among you. Are you the one who started the prison break?"

Player: "Yes, I am {playername}."

Mancinellus: "A pleasure to meet you my friend! I am Mancinellus. My father was a Roman centurio and my mother a Celtiberian peasant woman. I served as vigilia in the garrison of Augusta until I retired. When I heard that Wlodowiecus was recruiting for an expedition in the East I decided to join it... and good thing I did! Hah!"

Wlodowiecus: "If I had known the troubles you would cause I would have punched you in the face the day you approached me; but I must admit now you are saving our lives. Will you travel with us back to Rome, Mancinellus?"

Mancinellus: "Of course I will! The land East of the river Indus is terrible! Their gods must be the cruellest if you aren't even allowed to slaughter a cow! How can they live without beef!?"

Hadrianus: "I will of course return to Rome as well. With the money I will get from selling the spices I will buy a weapon and armor smithy and produce Hellenic equipment. This was always my dream."

Player: "Are you a Greek? I wondered why you are wearing a linothorax. They are rarely used these days."

Hadrianus: "Oh, I am not a Greek, but I love everything which comes from Greece! I am actually a Lugian by birth. The Lugians are Germanic people living in the East of Germania Magna. When I was a child, a Greek merchant visited our village. He was the first Greek who ever visited the Lugian lands. I was just a ten-year-old boy and was fascinated by his culture and, against better judgment, decided to hide myself on his carriage. It wasn't a smart idea, but I had luck: He discovered me and decided to adopt me. He taught me Greek and I taught him my language. We traveled together through the Roman Empire: From the North to the South and from the West to the East. When I grew older I decided to leave his service and bought myself a small shop in Rome, dedicated mainly to Greek culture. Then I heard about Wlodowiecus expedition and I thought it would be a good opportunity to learn more about the Greeks living far in the East."

Player: "Fascinating, I think I never met a Hellenophil Lugian! Quite the company you got here Wlodowiecus! However, I think it's time to leave this stinky cave."

Mancinellus: "Agreed, you three need a bath and some food! Follow me."
