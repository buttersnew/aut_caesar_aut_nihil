Speakers:

Extracted dialogue:

Wlodowiecus: "Hey, hey you! You are different from the others here! Can you understand me?"

Player: "Yes, I can. Who are you?"

Wlodowiecus: "I am Wlodowiecus."

Player: "Wlodowiecus? Olivarius has sent me to look for you. What happened? Why are you behind bars?"

Wlodowiecus: "A lot of shit happened! When we arrived at the town, Mancinellus, one of my companions, killed a cow! I told him the people here believe cows are holy and one shouldn't kill them but then they quickly imprisoned us for killing a holy animal. We were then brought to their queen, Karishma, and Mancinellus threw himself to her feet, saying some nonsense that he never seen someone more beautiful than her and continued to shower her with compliments and kissed her feet. He said he would worship her from now on, because she is a goddess. Karishma ordered her men to bring him to a special prison and I don't know what happened to him. She then said that we will be sent to work in a quarry as slaves. Quite ironic, because it is the exact same quarry where the marble I was searching is mined."

Hadrianus: "I expected this much to happen to be honest. By the way, I am Hadrianus. I joined Wlodowiecus' company because it sounded like a nice adventure... but I should have stayed home in Rome."

Player: "When I arrived at the city, I was informed that their queen took a white western man as a lover. I originally thought it was you, but now I believe it must be this Mancinellus person you speak of."

Wlodowiecus: "Mancinellus, Karishma's lover? Hmm... that could be possible. Have you tried to ask for an audience with her?"
