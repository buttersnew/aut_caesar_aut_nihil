Main accepted path:

Olivarius: "You! Do you bring news from the expedition?"

Player: "Expedition? No, I was just looking around."

Olivarius: "By Jupiter. The last message I got from the expedition was several months ago. I fear that they have failed."

Player: "What exactly happened? What expedition?"

Olivarius: "Ah! Well, I am a sculptor! And the cultists of Mithras ordered from me a statue of their god. They wanted special marble, which is said to be only found in a land East of the river Indus. My good friend Wlodowiecus volunteered to bring me this marble. I was stupid enough to agree to this risky plan and invested nearly all of my money into it. Now nearly half a year has passed and they still haven't returned."

Player: "I could try to find your friend."

Olivarius: "That would be great if you can! But sadly, I don't have enough money to pay you. However, if you return with enough marble, I could make you another statue as well."

Olivarius: "You want to help finding out what happened to the expedition of Wlodowiecus? I would make you another copy of the statue of Mithras as reward."

Player: "Agreed. I will help you find this lost expedition."

Olivarius: "Thank you very much Dominus. The last message, I received from Wlodowiecus is that he arrived in a city called Sagala. Though, I have no idea where this is. It would be best if you travel to Ctesiphon and ask some of the Merchants there. I know the city is frequented by eastern merchants. Maybe, you could join a caravan heading East there. Good luck and farewell."
