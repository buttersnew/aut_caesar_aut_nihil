# The Adventures of Wlodowiecus (I)

This folder extracts quest qst_wlodowiecus_adventure_1 from only two source files:
- module_system/module_dialogs.py
- module_system/module_game_menus.py

Ordering note:
- The files below follow the intended quest flow.
- The Sagala guard refusal can happen before or after the first prison conversation with Wlodowiecus, but the prison break only becomes available once the guard refusal has been addressed in the dialogue logic.

Files in reading order:
- 00_characters.md - participating characters and inferred origins/details from these two files only.
- 01_dialog_olivarius_quest_start.md - Olivarius introduces the expedition and starts the quest.
- 02_dialog_magister_civium_ctesiphon.md - the magister civium in Ctesiphon directs the player to Phamanus's caravan.
- 03_menu_travel_to_sagala.md - the caravan journey east.
- 04_menu_arrival_in_sagala.md - arrival in Sagala and first quest notes.
- 05_dialog_wlodowiecus_in_prison.md - first direct contact with Wlodowiecus and Hadrianus.
- 06_dialog_sagala_guard_refusal.md - the guard refuses an audience with Queen Karishma.
- 07_dialog_wlodowiecus_prison_break.md - the follow-up prison dialogue and the prison-break decision.
- 08_menu_escaped_sagala.md - the escape narrative after the breakout.
- 09_dialog_mancinellus_reunion.md - Mancinellus explains what happened and the survivors plan the return.
- 10_menu_return_from_india.md - the return journey and quest-state advance.
- 11_dialog_olivarius_quest_completion.md - Olivarius rewards the player and ends the quest.
- 12_menu_temple_of_mithras_unlock.md - aftermath unlock in Rome.
- progress_log.md - continuation log with scope, decisions, and processed source blocks.
