# Characters for The Adventures of Wlodowiecus (I)

Scope note:
- These descriptions use only module_system/module_dialogs.py and module_system/module_game_menus.py.
- Where origin is not stated directly, it is marked as an inference.

Named participants:

1. Olivarius
Origin: Roman, based in Rome.
Details: A sculptor whose business depends on obtaining special marble from lands east of the river Indus. He gives the quest, promises a copy of a Mithras statue as reward, and completes the quest by rewarding the player and helping establish the Temple of Mithras in Rome.

2. Wlodowiecus
Origin: Probably Germanic or from Germania, inferred from his sacrifices to Germanic gods and the way other characters speak about him.
Details: The missing expedition leader. He went east to obtain the special marble for Olivarius. In Sagala he and his companions are imprisoned after Mancinellus kills a holy cow. He leads the breakout effort once the player helps him.

3. Hadrianus
Origin: Lugian by birth. He explicitly says the Lugians are a Germanic people living east of Germania Magna.
Details: A member of Wlodowiecus's expedition. He is a Christian, hellenophil Lugian, wears Hellenic equipment, and explains that a Greek merchant adopted him and taught him Greek. He later says he wants to use his profits to open a weapon and armor smithy producing Hellenic equipment.

4. Mancinellus
Origin: Mixed Roman and Celtiberian background. He says his father was a Roman centurio and his mother a Celtiberian peasant woman.
Details: A retired vigilia from the garrison of Augusta. His behavior in Sagala triggers the expedition's imprisonment: he kills a holy cow, then flatters Queen Karishma and becomes her lover. Later he secretly contacts Emperor Kujula Kadphises, survives the fall of Sagala, secures the marble and spices, and rejoins the group in the cave.

5. Phamanus
Origin: An eastern merchant, described by the magister civium as a merchant from the far east.
Details: He leads the caravan from Ctesiphon toward Sagala and makes the expedition's outward journey possible.

6. Queen Karishma
Origin: Ruler of Sagala.
Details: She is described as a dark-skinned queen who rules Sagala and refuses audiences. Wlodowiecus says she condemns the imprisoned group to quarry labor after the holy-cow incident. Mancinellus says she kept him as her lover and imprisoned him in her room.

7. Emperor Kujula Kadphises
Origin: Emperor of the Kushan Empire.
Details: Mentioned in the Sagala arrival menu and later in Mancinellus's explanation. He besieges Sagala, receives Mancinellus's secret message, captures the city, spares Mancinellus and Karishma, and provides the caravan and spoils that allow the survivors to return west.

Supporting but unnamed participants:

1. The magister civium of Ctesiphon
Origin: Civic official in Ctesiphon.
Details: He tells the player about Phamanus and directs the player to the departing caravan.

2. The Sagala guards
Origin: Local guards in Sagala; the talk block uses Indian guard troops.
Details: They refuse to grant the player an audience with Queen Karishma and thereby force the quest toward the prison-break solution.
