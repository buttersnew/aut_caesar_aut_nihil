Here is the complete weapons list for your mid-1st century AD setting, divided strictly into **Melee Weapons** and **Ranged Weapons**.

To honor your constraint that the **Falx** and **Kontos** are the *only* two-handed melee weapons in the game, standard spears and longswords do not have the "Versatile" property. This perfectly reflects the era's combat, which heavily relied on keeping a shield in one hand at all times.

### MELEE WEAPONS
*(Weapons used for hand-to-hand combat. Some possess the "Thrown" property, allowing them to be hurled at the enemy.)*

*   **Seax / Sica / Pugio (Dagger)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d4 Piercing (Pugio) or Slashing (Seax/Sica)
    *   **Properties:** Finesse, Light, Thrown (Range 20/60)
    *   *Details:* The *Pugio* is the broad Roman sidearm. The *Sica* is the curved Thracian/assassin blade. The *Seax* is a single-edged Germanic knife.
*   **Club**
    *   **Damage:** 1d4 Bludgeoning
    *   **Properties:** Light
    *   *Details:* Simple wooden cudgels used by the poorest tribal levies or hastily armed peasants.
*   **Generic Short Sword**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Slashing
    *   **Properties:** Finesse, Light
    *   *Details:* Iron short swords used by Celtic, Iberian (like the *Falcata*), and Germanic warriors. Designed primarily for slashing and hacking in skirmishes.
*   **Gladius (Specialized Stabbing Sword)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Finesse, Light, *Special*
    *   *Details:* The iconic Roman weapon, perfectly engineered for thrusting from behind a large shield. **Special:** Because of its devastating efficiency in tight spaces, attacks made with a Gladius against a target that is Grappled or Restrained score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
*   **Spatha**
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 Slashing
    *   **Properties:** None (One-Handed)
    *   *Details:* A longer iron sword (around 30 inches). Originally used by Celtic chieftains and Roman cavalry so riders could reach infantry on the ground, but becoming more popular with infantry commanders.
*   **One-Handed Axe**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Slashing
    *   **Properties:** Light, Thrown (Range 20/60)
    *   *Details:* Covers everything from simple Germanic wood-axes pressed into war, to the specialized, balanced cavalry axes used by Eastern Parthian riders.
*   **One-Handed Mace**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Bludgeoning
    *   **Properties:** None (One-Handed)
    *   *Details:* A heavy iron or bronze flanged head on a wooden shaft. Rare in Western Europe, but heavily utilized by Eastern factions like the Parthians and Caucasians to crush armored foes.
*   **Spear (Hasta / Dory)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Thrown (Range 20/60)
    *   *Details:* The standard, one-handed thrusting spear used by Greek hoplites, Roman Triarii, and tribal shield-walls.
*   **Light Throwing Spear (Verutum)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Thrown (Range 30/120), Light
    *   *Details:* A short, thin javelin used by skirmishers (*Velites* or tribal youths). Because it is Light, you can easily hold a bundle of them in your shield hand.
*   **Heavy Throwing Spear (Framea / Lancea)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Thrown (Range 15/30)
    *   *Details:* The primary weapon of the Germanic warrior. Thicker and heavier than a standard spear. It lacks range when thrown, but hits with massive impact.
*   **Pilum**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Thrown (Range 20/60), *Special*
    *   *Details:* The Roman heavy javelin, featuring a long iron shank meant to bend upon impact. **Special (Shield-Breaker):** If you hit an enemy wielding a shield, the Pilum gets stuck. The enemy loses their Shield AC bonus (+2) until they use an Action to pull it free.
*   **Falx**
    *   **Damage:** 1d10 Slashing
    *   **Properties:** Heavy, Two-Handed, *Special*
    *   *Details:* A terrifying Dacian/Thracian weapon with a curved blade sharpened on the inside edge, swung with massive force to pull down shields and cleave helmets. **Special:** Attacks made with a Falx ignore the +2 AC bonus granted by shields.
*   **Kontos**
    *   **Damage:** 1d10 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Heavy, Two-Handed, Reach
    *   *Details:* A massive (13+ foot) cavalry lance used by Parthian and Sarmatian *Cataphracts* (heavy shock cavalry). Wielded with two hands from horseback to impale enemies before wheeling away.

### RANGED WEAPONS
*(Projectile weapons that require ammunition to fire. They rely on Dexterity and cannot be used for melee attacks.)*

*   **Sling (Funda)**
    *   **Damage:** 1d4 Bludgeoning (or 1d6 if using cast-lead *glandes* bullets)
    *   **Properties:** Ammunition (Range 30/120)
    *   *Details:* A strip of leather used to hurl stones or lead bullets. Devastatingly effective in ancient warfare; elite slingers from the Balearic Islands or Rhodes are highly prized mercenaries.
*   **Short Bow**
    *   **Damage:** 1d6 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Ammunition (Range 80/320), Two-Handed
    *   *Details:* A simple self-bow carved from a single piece of wood (yew or ash). Common among Celtic and Germanic hunters, cheap, and reliable in the damp European weather.
*   **Longbow**
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Ammunition (Range 150/600), Heavy, Two-Handed
    *   *Details:* A massive wooden bow (like the Germanic Nydam bows). Because of the "Heavy" property, it is very difficult for small creatures to use, and historically impossible to fire effectively from horseback.
*   **Composite Bow**
    *   **Damage:** 1d8 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Ammunition (Range 150/600), Two-Handed
    *   *Details:* An advanced recurve bow made of layered wood, horn, and sinew. Brought west by Eastern auxiliaries (Syrians, Cretans). It hits as hard as a Longbow but lacks the "Heavy" property, making it the supreme weapon for mounted horse-archers.
*   **Manuballista**
    *   **Damage:** 1d10 Piercing
    *   **Properties:** Ammunition (Range 100/400), Heavy, Two-Handed, Loading
    *   *Details:* A heavy, handheld torsion-powered crossbow. An advanced piece of Roman military engineering, incredibly rare and usually restricted to sieges or elite legionary engineers. It fires heavy iron bolts capable of punching straight through armor.
