### SHIELDS
*(Equipment worn on the off-hand to deflect blows and missiles. Only one shield can be wielded at a time. Remember: A struck **Pilum** strips a shield's AC bonus, and a **Falx** ignores it entirely.)*

#### LIGHT SHIELDS
*(Favored by skirmishers and light cavalry. They do not hinder movement and allow the user to easily carry other items.)*

*   **Parma / Caetra (Light Round Shield)**
    *   **Armor Class Bonus:** +1 AC
    *   **Properties:** Light
    *   *Details:* The *Parma* is a small Roman buckler (about 3 feet across) used by standard-bearers (*Signiferi*), cavalry, and light infantry. The *Caetra* is an Iberian/Celtic equivalent. **Special:** Because it is so light and maneuverable, wielding this shield leaves your hand free enough to hold (but not attack with) small items, such as a torch or a bundle of *Verutum* (light throwing spears).
*   **Pelta (Crescent Skirmisher Shield)**
    *   **Armor Class Bonus:** +1 AC
    *   **Properties:** Light
    *   *Details:* A lightweight, crescent-shaped shield made of wicker or wood covered in goat skin, heavily favored by Thracian skirmishers. Cutouts on the top edge allow the user to see and throw javelins while staying protected. **Special:** You do not suffer disadvantage on Ranged Attack rolls made with throwing spears while wielding this shield.

#### STANDARD SHIELDS
*(Large, flat, and made of wood, used by tribal warriors, soldiers of eastern kingdoms and auxiliary troops.)*

*   **Barbarian Shield**
    *   **Armor Class Bonus:** +2 AC
    *   **Properties:** None
    *   *Details:* Shape is typically flat oval, but it can be also hexagonal (for Germanic and Celtic types), but also more exotic shapes can be found across the world. The standard shield of the Celtic and Germanic warrior. Large, flat, and made of oak or linden wood, featuring an iron or bronze boss in the center to protect the hand. It is "center-gripped" (held by a single handle behind the boss), meaning it can be punched forward aggressively in combat.
*   **Auxiliary Scutum**
    *   **Armor Class Bonus:** +2 AC
    *   **Properties:** None
    *   *Details:* An oval Scutum, used by auxiliary troops and some light infantry. It is still large enough to provide solid protection, but it is more manageable for troops who need to be more mobile than the heavy legionaries. Can also be used on horseback by medium cavalry.

#### HEAVY SHIELDS
*(Massive shields meant for tight, disciplined formations. They offer unparalleled protection but are too heavy for skirmishing or stealth.)*

*   **Scutum (Legionary Tower Shield)**
    *   **Armor Class Bonus:** +2 AC
    *   **Properties:** Heavy, Strength 13 Required, Disadvantage on Stealth
    *   *Details:* The iconic Roman legionary shield. It is semi-cylindrical, meaning it wraps around the user's body. It is heavy, made of layered plywood covered in canvas and leather, and features a heavy bronze boss. It is engineered for the brutal, grinding push of the infantry line.
    *   **Special (Mobile Cover):** Because of its massive, curving size, the Scutum grants its wielder Half-Cover (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws) specifically against *Ranged Attacks* originating from in front of them.
