### SPECIAL EQUIPMENT (Mid-1st Century AD)
This list covers specialized military gear, medical tools, and "heroic" items that players might use for specific tactical advantages.

#### 1. MILITARY & SIEGE TOOLS
*   **Tribuli (Caltrops)**
    *   *Description:* Four-pointed iron spikes designed so that one point always faces up.
    *   *Mechanic:* As an action, you can scatter these over a 5ft square. Any creature entering the area must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or stop moving and take 1d4 piercing damage. Until the creature regains at least 1 hit point, its walking speed is reduced by 10 feet.
*   **Vitis (Centurion’s Vine Staff)**
    *   *Description:* A tough, knobby staff made of grapevine wood, the symbol of a Centurion’s rank. Used for "correcting" soldiers.
    *   *Mechanic:* When used as a weapon, it functions as a Club.
    *   **Special (Discipline):** If you are a leader, you can use a Bonus Action to strike an adjacent allied creature with the Vitis. The ally takes 1 bludgeoning damage but can immediately reroll a failed saving throw against being Frightened.
*   **The Signum (Unit Standard)**
    *   *Description:* A tall pole decorated with silver discs (*phalerae*) and a hand icon at the top. Carried by the *Signifer*.
    *   *Mechanic:* Requires one hand to hold. All allies within 30 feet who can see the Signum gain a +1 bonus to Wisdom and Charisma saving throws. If the Signifer falls and the Signum is captured, all allies within sight have Disadvantage on attack rolls until it is recovered.
*   **The Aquila (Eagle Standard)**
    *   *Description:* A large, ornate standard topped with a golden eagle. The most sacred symbol of the Roman Legion.
    *   *Mechanic:* Requires one hand to hold. All allies within 30 feet who can see the Aquila gain a +2 bonus to Wisdom and Charisma saving throws. If the Aquila is captured by the enemy, all allies within sight have Disadvantage on attack rolls until it is recovered.
*   **Cestus (Gladiatorial Spiked Glove)**
    *   *Description:* Leather strips wrapped around the hand and forearm, fitted with lead weights or iron spikes.
    *   *Mechanic:* 1d4 piercing/bludgeoning damage. Your unarmed strikes are considered lethal. You cannot use the "Light" property with a Cestus to make off-hand attacks unless you have the Dual Wielder feat.

#### 2. MEDICINE & ALCHEMY
*   **Acetum (Medicinal Vinegar/Posca)**
    *   *Description:* Sour wine or vinegar mixed with water and herbs. The standard drink of the Roman soldier.
    *   *Mechanic:* Drinking *Posca* during a Short Rest allows you to regain 1 additional hit point for every Hit Die spent, as it prevents heat stroke and provides clean hydration.
*   **Medicus Kit (Surgical Tools)**
    *   *Description:* Bronze scalpels, bone levers, hooks, and linen bandages soaked in wine (an early antiseptic).
    *   *Mechanic:* Grants Advantage on Wisdom (Medicine) checks to stabilize a dying creature or treat "Lingering Injuries."
*   **Woad (Celtic War Paint)**
    *   *Description:* A blue dye extracted from the *Isatis tinctoria* plant.
    *   *Mechanic:* Applying woad takes 10 minutes and lasts for 8 hours. It grants Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks.
    *   **Special:** Some tribes believe it numbs pain; you gain 2 Temporary Hit Points at the start of combat, but only if you are wearing no body armor.

#### 3. FIELD ARTILLERY (Portable)
*   **Scorpio (Bolt-Shooter)**
    *   *Description:* A smaller, tripod-mounted version of the Ballista. Two men can carry it. It is a torsion-powered sniper rifle of the ancient world.
    *   *Mechanic:* **Damage:** 2d10 Piercing. **Range:** 200/800. **Properties:** Heavy, Loading (requires 1 Action to fire, 1 Action to reload). Usually operated by a crew of two to fire every round.
*   **Carcass (Incendiary Pitch)**
    *   *Description:* A ceramic pot filled with pine resin, sulfur, and saltpeter.
    *   *Mechanic:* Can be thrown (20/60) or fired from a manuballista. On impact, it shatters and ignites. The target takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end this damage by using its action to make a DC 10 Dexterity check to extinguish the flames.

#### 4. UTILITY & LUXURY
*   **Cera (Wax Tablets & Stylus)**
    *   *Description:* Wooden boards filled with black wax. The standard "notebook" for Roman officers and merchants.
    *   *Mechanic:* Allows for silent communication and record-keeping. The wax can be smoothed over with the flat end of the stylus to "delete" messages instantly, making it perfect for spies.
*   **Phalerae (Military Medals)**
    *   *Description:* Large, embossed metal discs worn on a leather harness over the chest.
    *   *Mechanic:* Each *Phalera* represents a specific heroic deed. They grant a +1 bonus to Charisma (Persuasion) checks when interacting with Roman soldiers of lower rank.
*   **The Draco (Dacian/Sarmatian Wind-Sack)**
    *   *Description:* A bronze wolf’s head with a long, fabric "tail" that hisses in the wind when the rider gallops.
    *   *Mechanic:* When the bearer moves at least 30 feet on a mount, the Draco emits a terrifying howling sound. All enemies within 20 feet must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or be distracted, allowing the rider to move out of their reach without provoking Attacks of Opportunity.
*   **Warrior Bracelets (Armlets)**
    *   *Description:* Decorative metal bands worn on the upper arms. They are a status symbol among tribal warriors and can be engraved with personal or tribal symbols.
    *   *Mechanic:* Wearing a pair of matching bracelets grants you a +1 bonus to AC against attacks made by creatures that can see the bracelets, as they are distracted by the gleam and craftsmanship.

#### 5. LEGENDARY ODDITIES & COMIC RELICS
*(Unique treasures, bizarre curiosities, and joke-relics. Some are genuinely valuable. Others are mostly useful for causing confusion, scandal, or a very memorable feast scene.)*

*   **Golden Bong**
    *   *Description:* A Scythian gold water pipe, apparently meant for smoking substances that leave warriors "high and happy" before battle.
    *   *Mechanic:* During a Short Rest, up to 3 willing creatures can share it. For the next hour, they gain Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened, but suffer Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.
*   **Roses of Egypt**
    *   *Description:* An outrageously luxurious perfume, so rich in floral and citrus notes that entering a room with it becomes a tactical decision.
    *   *Mechanic:* Applying it grants Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Performance) checks in courts, feasts, and other civilized gatherings. You suffer Disadvantage on Stealth checks made to move unnoticed among nearby people, because everyone can smell you coming.
*   **Golden Lyre**
    *   *Description:* Nero's own golden lyre, complete with absurd prestige and enough artistic vanity to fill an amphitheater.
    *   *Mechanic:* Grants Advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks involving music. Roman elites are also less likely to openly mock a poor performance from the owner of such an extravagant instrument.
*   **Pilos**
    *   *Description:* A restored Ancient Greek Pilos helmet (a simple, unadorned bronze cone, it offers nearly total 360-degree vision and hearing), revered by its admirers as the ultimate symbol of power. Glory to PILOS.
    *   *Mechanic:* Counts as a respectable medium helmet. Once per day, after loudly invoking PILOS, you may reroll a failed Charisma (Intimidation) or Charisma (Performance) check.
*   **Stinky Fur**
    *   *Description:* A rank old pelt that might look primal and fearsome from a distance, but becomes far less impressive once anyone catches the smell.
    *   *Mechanic:* Grants Advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against superstitious peasants or raw recruits, but Disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks in civilized settlements.
*   **Cupid's Arrow**
    *   *Description:* The legendary arrow of Cupid, intended less for battlefield efficiency than for immediate romantic catastrophe.
    *   *Mechanic:* Best treated as a plot device. If it successfully pricks a target and the myth is allowed to work, the victim becomes infatuated with the first person they see for the rest of the scene.
*   **Cup of Chrestos**
    *   *Description:* A perfectly ordinary cup with a gloriously undercutting backstory: it likely belonged to some man named Chrestos.
    *   *Mechanic:* Worth little on its own, but if you can convince the gullible that it is a holy relic, you gain Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks involving pilgrims, treasure-hunters, or collectors.
*   **The Art of Poop, by Poopianus**
    *   *Description:* A scholarly work devoted entirely to the highest questions of filth: whether one should do a handstand while pooping, what poop tastes like, and how exactly it smells. Its author, Poopianus, is presented as the undisputed master of all poop.
    *   *Mechanic:* After spending 10 minutes studying it, you gain Advantage on Constitution saving throws against foul smells, filth, and other forms of revolting stench for the next 24 hours. Reading passages aloud in polite company imposes Disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks.
