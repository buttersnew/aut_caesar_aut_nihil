### HEAVY SIEGE MOUNTS
*(Extremely rare in mainland Europe, used strictly by Emperors, elite generals, or Eastern kings for psychological warfare and breaking infantry lines.)*

*   **North African Forest Elephant**
    *   **Speed:** 40 ft.
    *   **Carrying Capacity:** 1,300 lbs.
    *   *Details:* This is the breed famously used by Hannibal and later monopolized by Rome (though they were sadly being hunted to near-extinction in the arenas by the 1st century AD). They are slightly smaller than modern African bush elephants (around 8 feet at the shoulder). They are usually ridden bareback by a single driver and used to trample light infantry.
*   **Indian War Elephant**
    *   **Speed:** 40 ft.
    *   **Carrying Capacity:** 2,000+ lbs.
    *   *Details:* Massive beasts imported from the East via trade routes, sometimes used by Parthian kings or kept in the Imperial Roman menagerie. Due to their immense size, they are large enough to carry a wooden tower on their backs.
    *   **Mechanic (Psychological Terror):** Horses are naturally terrified of the scent and sound of elephants. Any cavalry horse or chariot pony that has not been specifically trained to face elephants must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw when coming within 60 feet of one, or become Frightened and attempt to flee.
    *   **Mechanic (The Rampage):** The greatest historical weakness of the war elephant. If the elephant takes more than 15 damage in a single turn, the driver must make a DC 14 Animal Handling check. On a failure, the elephant panics, entering a rampage. On its next turn, it will move in a random direction and attack the nearest creature—friend or foe.

### ELEPHANT TACK & GEAR
*(Specialized equipment required to field these living siege engines.)*

*   **The Howdah (Elephant Tower)**
    *   **Capacity:** 1 Driver (on the neck), up to 3 Ranged Attackers (in the tower)
    *   **Cost:** Exorbitant
    *   *Details:* A reinforced wooden box strapped to the back of a large Indian elephant, functioning as a mobile guard tower.
    *   **Mechanic:** Occupants inside the Howdah gain **Three-Quarters Cover** (+5 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws) against attacks originating from the ground. Furthermore, because of the height, they ignore half-cover when shooting arrows or throwing javelins down at infantry.
*   **The Mahout’s Chisel and Mallet**
    *   **Cost:** Cheap
    *   *Details:* A grim but historically accurate piece of equipment. Every elephant driver (in India called *Mahout*) carried a heavy iron chisel and a mallet.
    *   **Mechanic:** If a war elephant panics and begins trampling its own allied troops, the driver can use an Action to drive the chisel into the base of the elephant's spine, instantly killing the beast to save the army from destruction.
*   **Tusk Swords & Iron Caps**
    *   **Cost:** Expensive
    *   *Details:* Heavy iron caps or spiked extensions fitted over the tips of the elephant's tusks. Originally an Eastern invention, they turn the already devastating sweeping attack of an elephant into a lethal, armor-piercing strike.
    *   **Mechanic:** Changes the elephant's tusk attack from bludgeoning damage to slashing or piercing damage, and grants a +1 bonus to damage rolls against armored infantry.
